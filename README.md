# Level 1 High-Power Rocketry Project

Ongoing high-power rocketry project focused on preparing for a Tripoli Level 1 certification flight through rocket design, simulation, construction, and launch planning.

## Key Skills & Tools

- **OpenRocket Simulation:** Modeled rocket stability, CG/CP relationship, altitude, velocity, and motor performance
- **Rocket Design:** Learned airframe layout, fin configuration, motor mounts, recovery systems, and component placement
- **High-Power Rocket Construction:** Researching epoxy bonding, structural reinforcement, motor retention, parachute recovery, and launch preparation
- **Flight Planning:** Evaluating motor selection, descent rate, and stability margins
- **Tripoli Rocketry Association:** Joined TRA and preparing to complete the Level 1 certification process
- **Music City Missile Club (MC2):** Exploring local launch opportunities and certification support

## Current Progress

Currently preparing to build and simulate the certification rocket before completing a high-power launch attempt.
