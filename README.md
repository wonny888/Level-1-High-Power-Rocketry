# 🚀 Level 1 High-Power Rocketry Project

Ongoing high-power rocketry project focused on preparing for a **Tripoli Level 1 certification flight** through rocket design, simulation, construction, and launch planning.

## Key Skills & Tools

- **OpenRocket Simulation:** Modeled rocket stability, CG/CP relationship, altitude, velocity, and motor performance
- **Rocket Design:** Evaluated airframe layout, fin configuration, motor mounts, recovery systems, and component placement
- **High-Power Rocket Construction:** Purchased and began modifying a rocket kit, including **laser-cutting and trimming replacement fins to improve fit and alignment**
- **Structural Assembly:** Preparing epoxy bonding, motor retention, fin installation, airframe assembly, and parachute recovery hardware
- **Flight Planning:** Evaluating motor selection, descent rate, stability margins, and launch configuration
- **Tripoli Rocketry Association:** Joined TRA and preparing to complete the Level 1 certification process
- **Music City Missile Club (MC2):** Exploring local launch opportunities and certification support

## Current Progress

Currently assembling and modifying the certification rocket, refining fin fit and alignment, validating the design in **OpenRocket**, and preparing the recovery and motor systems before completing the Level 1 certification flight attempt.

## Technologies

OpenRocket, Laser Cutting, High-Power Rocketry, Mechanical Assembly, Fabrication, Stability Analysis, Recovery Systems
